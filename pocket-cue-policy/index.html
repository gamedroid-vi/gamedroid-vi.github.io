Pocket Cue Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how Pocket Cue handles information on Android (package com.pocket.cue) and iOS/iPadOS (bundle ID com.pocketcue.lv). It applies to both versions unless a platform-specific difference is stated below. For privacy questions, contact trang.lth610@gmail.com.

Game data

Pocket Cue does not require an account. Game progress, achievements, player names entered in the game, preferences and the selected age group are saved in the app's storage. We do not operate a server that receives this game data. The app does not ask for your exact date of birth. Android, iOS and iPadOS may include app data in device or cloud backups, depending on your device, account and backup settings. We do not provide our own cloud synchronization service.

Advertising

Pocket Cue uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements at breaks after completed games. Ads may be requested for any age group, subject to the applicable privacy choices. For players below 18 or whose age group is unknown, the app applies child-directed and under-age-of-consent ad treatment. The app requests ads with a maximum content rating of G for all players. These are conservative advertising settings and do not change the game's intended audience. Both versions use Google's advertising SDK without additional mediation networks.

Players can use the game without an internet connection; an unavailable advertisement does not prevent continuing the game. Choosing not to consent may result in limited advertising or no advertising, depending on Google's response and applicable requirements.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate general location), device or app identifiers, app/ad interactions, advertising data and diagnostic information such as crash and performance information. The identifiers available depend on the platform, device settings, SDK configuration and applicable permissions. On Android, these may include the Android advertising ID and app set ID. On iOS/iPadOS, these may include app- or developer-scoped device identifiers; access to the advertising identifier (IDFA) requires Apple's App Tracking Transparency authorization. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Personalized advertising depends on applicable choices and requirements; choosing non-personalized ads does not necessarily prevent all data processing.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information for details about Google's handling of information, retention and controls.

iOS and iPadOS advertising settings

The current iOS/iPadOS version requests non-personalized advertisements, disables Google's publisher first-party ID feature and does not initiate an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission request to obtain the IDFA. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no information is collected: Google's SDK may still process information for ad delivery, contextual advertising, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention as described above.

For details about the iOS SDK, see Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures. Apple's general tracking controls are available under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. An app that has not requested tracking permission may not appear in that list. These controls do not disable all processing by advertising SDKs.

Your choices

Open the game's settings using the gear button, then select Privacy. You can view this policy, update your age group and, where required, reopen Ad privacy choices. Where applicable, Google User Messaging Platform presents consent options before advertising requests are permitted. On Android, you may also reset or delete your advertising ID using the advertising privacy controls in device settings, where available. On iPhone and iPad, you can review tracking permissions in the system settings described above. The available controls and wording may vary by operating system version.

Children and teens

Pocket Cue is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. The current version uses restricted, child-directed ad treatment for anyone selecting an age group below 18 or leaving the age group unknown. These users are not served personalized advertising through this configuration. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information on a developer-operated server. A parent or guardian with a concern can contact the email address above.

Retention, deletion and contact

Local game data remains until removed from the device. On Android, you can clear the app's storage through device settings or uninstall the app. On iOS/iPadOS, delete the app to remove its local app data; offloading an app retains its documents and data. Device or cloud backups, if enabled, are managed separately through your Android/Google or Apple account and backup settings, and restoring a backup may restore saved game data. We cannot directly erase information processed independently by Google; use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for that information. If you contact us by email, we receive the address and information you include and use it to respond to your request. You may contact us to request deletion of correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app or its information practices change. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page.
